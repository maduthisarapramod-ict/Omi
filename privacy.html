Privacy Policy for OMI Royal

Effective Date: November 29, 2026

Developer: Pramod Maduthisara

OMI Royal ("we", "our", or "the app") is a traditional Sri Lankan card game designed for entertainment. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Player Display Name & Preferences: Any nickname you enter and your language selection (Sinhala, English, or Tamil) are stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage) solely to improve your gameplay experience.
  • Multiplayer Gameplay Data: When using the "Play with friends" online mode, temporary session identifiers, room codes, card moves, and in-game chat messages are transmitted in real time via public MQTT relay brokers (such as EMQX, HiveMQ, or Mosquitto) strictly to synchronize the game between players. We do not permanently store chat logs or gameplay history on any server.

2. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information. The app does not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or location permissions. External resources used include Google Fonts for typography and public MQTT brokers for real-time multiplayer communication.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored preferences and active room sessions remain only on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage/cache or uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

OMI Royal does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

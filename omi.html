How to play
  • 4 players, 2 teams. You and the player opposite you are partners.
  • 32 cards (7 to Ace). Everyone gets 4 cards first, then the player who plays first chooses the Omi (trump suit). Then 4 more cards each.
  • You must follow the suit that was led. If you can't, play any card – a trump card wins!
  • Highest trump wins the trick; if no trump, the highest card of the led suit wins. Winner leads next.
  • Team with 5+ of the 8 tricks wins 1 point. Winning all 8 (Kapothi) gives 2 points. A 4–4 tie adds a bonus point to the next hand.
  • First team to 10 points wins.
